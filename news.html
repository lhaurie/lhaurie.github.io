Monthly Highlight

Joseph Louis Lagrange
18th-century mathematician and physicist who helped reformulate classical mechanics into what we now call Lagrangian mechanics, a cornerstone of theoretical physics. He was basically a mathematical powerhouse who turned dynamics into a clean variational framework, laying groundwork that modern physics (from quantum field theory to general relativity) still leans on heavily.
