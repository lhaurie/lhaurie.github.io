Monthly Highlight

Joseph Liouville
French mathematician and PhD student of Poisson. He made a lot of great contributions to number theory and complex analysis. In physics, he is remember for his theorem that had a tremendous impact on statistical physics and Hamilton mechanics: the phase space volume of a conserved quantity is constant.
